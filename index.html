Gravity Says No stats

Gravity Says No stats is a small private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Gravity Says No (wordless physics-challenge Shorts).

It reads the channel's own YouTube Analytics (views, watch time, audience retention, shares, subscribers) with read-only access, so the owner can see which videos people enjoy and make better ones. It cannot upload, edit or delete anything, and it is not offered to other users.
