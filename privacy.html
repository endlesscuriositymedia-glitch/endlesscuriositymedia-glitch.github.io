Privacy policy — Gravity Says No stats

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Who uses this app

Gravity Says No stats is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Gravity Says No to read that channel's own statistics. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission (Google scope yt-analytics.readonly), it reads YouTube Analytics reports for the owner's own channel: views, engaged views, watch time, audience retention, likes, shares, comments and subscriber counts per video. It also reads public video information (titles, lengths, publish dates) through the YouTube Data API.

How the data is used and stored

The data is stored only on the owner's own computer and used only to improve the channel's videos. It is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. The app has read-only access and cannot change anything on YouTube.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also complies with the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored data can be deleted by the owner at any time.
